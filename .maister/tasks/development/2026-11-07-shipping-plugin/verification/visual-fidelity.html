High fidelity. Of 10 design references (8 screens, 2 components), 9 match structurally, 1 deviates in a minor way, and none drifts.

The one deviation, screen:shipping-config-edit, is required by the spec: the mockup shows a carrier row and a method row in edit mode together, while req 4 allows only one edit row. Other differences are sample data or ordering the spec overrides, or mockup-only helper classes (row-off, muted) the spec chose not to reproduce.

Key Decisions

  • Spec-sanctioned differences (row order, hint content, no row-off/muted) count as a match and are noted per screen.
  • Mockups were rendered in the same browser and their .tc-plugin region was captured, for a like-for-like comparison.

Open Questions / Risks

  • Inactive rows are not visually de-emphasised; only the badges carry the state. Design may want a tc-* class later.
  • Both the mockups and the implementation render <p class="tc-error"> grey because of .tc-plugin p specificity (e2e Minor #2).
